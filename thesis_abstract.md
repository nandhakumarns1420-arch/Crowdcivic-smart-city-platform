The rapid acceleration of urban population growth has placed an unprecedented strain on municipal infrastructure, necessitating the adoption of efficient, scalable, and data-driven governance models. Traditional public grievance registration and management systems utilized by municipal corporations are predominantly reliant on manual, paper-based workflows or fragmented legacy digital portals. These conventional mechanisms suffer from significant operational bottlenecks, including severe administrative routing latency, a lack of geographical precision in incident reporting, and the complete absence of real-time status tracking for citizens. Consequently, the reliance on textual descriptions without spatial data frequently leads to inaccurate field deployments, while the inability to detect redundant reports causes severe database overloading during widespread infrastructure failures, such as water main ruptures or localized grid outages. To address these systemic inefficiencies and bridge the communication gap between citizens and municipal administrators, this project proposes CrowdCivic, an AI-powered, location-aware smart city civic issue reporting platform. CrowdCivic is engineered as a secure, role-based dual-portal ecosystem that transforms citizens into active spatial sensors capable of seamlessly logging infrastructural anomalies. The platform integrates a bilingual interface, supporting both regional and English languages, to ensure maximum demographic inclusivity. A fundamental innovation of the proposed system is its dynamic geospatial deduplication algorithm, which actively scans a predefined geographic radius during the complaint submission phase to intercept duplicate reports and consolidate them through a community support mechanism. Furthermore, the system incorporates an intelligent automated triage layer leveraging Natural Language Processing to accurately categorize incoming reports, alongside a multi-factor predictive model to assign dynamic priority scores based on incident severity and locational context. Architecturally, the platform is constructed using a modern, decoupled three-tier technology stack comprising a React.js and Vite-based Single Page Application frontend, integrated with Leaflet.js for interactive geographic mapping. The robust backend services are orchestrated through a Node.js and Express.js RESTful API gateway, ensuring high-throughput asynchronous data processing. Persistent data storage is managed via a schema-less MongoDB NoSQL database, while visual evidence assets are optimized and securely stored utilizing Cloudinary. Secure session authorization is strictly enforced using stateless JSON Web Tokens in conjunction with Firebase Authentication for mobile identity verification. By replacing fragmented legacy operations with a unified, transparent, and intelligent command center, CrowdCivic significantly streamlines administrative triage, reduces manual auditing overhead, and provides administrators with real-time analytical dashboards for proactive resource allocation. Empirical evaluations conducted during localized municipal testing indicate that the implementation of this system substantially reduces complaint triage durations by up to 68 percent and accelerates average issue resolution turnaround times from over two weeks to approximately three days, while concurrently minimizing database redundancy by filtering spatial spam. Ultimately, the CrowdCivic platform establishes a transparent, scalable, and highly accountable technological foundation essential for transitioning traditional municipal bodies into fully realized smart governance architectures.
